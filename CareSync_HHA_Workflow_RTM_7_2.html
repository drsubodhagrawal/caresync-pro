🔗 How the Network Works — Many-to-Many
🏥 Healthcare Enterprise
A physician group, hospital system, or ACO that refers patients out for home health services. They bill for physician-side services (CCM, RPM 99457, PCM, etc.).
Example: Athens Heart Group has 234 patients spread across 3 different home health agencies.
🏠 Home Health Agency (HHA)
A licensed home health agency that delivers care and bills TX Medicaid S9110. Can receive referrals from multiple enterprises simultaneously.
Example: ABC Home Health serves 187 patients from 4 different enterprises.
🔗 The Connection: Patient Referrals
Every patient is a referral record linking ONE Enterprise → ONE HHA. The same patient never appears twice. But the same Enterprise can refer to many HHAs, and the same HHA can receive from many Enterprises. This is the many-to-many relationship at the heart of the platform.

📋 Archive policy (HIPAA-compliant):
  • Archived users cannot log in. Their credentials are disabled.
  • Archived users cannot be assigned new patients, referrals, or tasks.
