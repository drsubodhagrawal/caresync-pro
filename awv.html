🩺 Annual Wellness Visit (AWV)
Athens Heart Center — Medicare AWV worklist. Initial visit bills G0438; subsequent annual visits bill G0439 (once per 12 months). Completing the required AWV elements also surfaces HCC recapture opportunities — every chronic problem reviewed with MEAT during the AWV can flow to the encounter.
🩺
The AWV is not a head-to-toe physical. It is a structured prevention visit: Health Risk Assessment, cognitive & depression screening, functional/fall-risk review, medication reconciliation, and a personalized prevention plan. Deductible & copay are $0 when billed under G0438 / G0439 with diagnosis Z00.00.
